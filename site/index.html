When can I retire?

You are financially independent when your investment returns can cover your annual expenses. However, growing your net worth with compound interest is slow. To retire in 5 or 10 years, the most important number is your savings rate. Learn more.

This calculator makes assumptions
  • Your expenses now equal your expenses in retirement
  • You will never draw down the principal. Your net worth will never shrink.
  • After tax income is what you earn after taxes
  • Annual return on investment is after taxes and inflation
