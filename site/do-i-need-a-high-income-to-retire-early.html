About these numbers

The same assumptions as the early retirement calculator: both start from nothing, earn 5% a year after inflation, and retire when 4% of their savings covers a year of spending. That means saving 25 times what you spend in a year.

Why income cancels out

Earning more lets you save more, but if you save the same share, you spend more too. A bigger lifestyle needs a bigger nest egg to pay for it. Save four times as much a year and you need four times as much in the end, so you get there at the same time.

What does help is a higher savings rate. It works twice: you put more away each year, and you need less, because you've learned to live on less.

What income does change

A higher income makes a high savings rate easier. Rent and food take most of a small paycheck, so saving half of $30,000 is much harder than saving half of $200,000. And the higher earner retires with a bigger cushion for surprises. But on the same savings rate, both people reach retirement at the same time.
